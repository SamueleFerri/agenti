# 5 – STRIPS Planning

Planning in the STRIPS tradition: states, goals and operators, the planner as a
search, and STRIPS's own algorithm—all of it in Prolog.

## Classes

| class | deck | what is in it |
|---|---|---|
| [`l8`](l8/) | *L8 – STRIPS Planning in Prolog* | planning as deduction; states, goals and operators; applying one; planners, depth-first and shortest-first; the Sussman anomaly; the UPS World; STRIPS's own algorithm |

## What you need

**A browser, and nothing else**, as for [`1-prolog`](../1-prolog/): paste a
program into the [2P-Kt web IDE](https://tuprolog.github.io/2p-kt/web-ide/) or
[SWISH](https://swish.swi-prolog.org), then ask a goal from the foot of it.
